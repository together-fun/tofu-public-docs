# Gifts & Red Envelopes

Make it rain. Reward the alpha. Celebrate together.

### Gifts

Send gifts to anyone in the room — the streamer who called the bottom, the anon who posted the alpha, or your clanmate who just got liquidated (as a joke... mostly).

<!-- IMG: gift dialog + on-screen gift animation screenshot -->

* **On-screen animations** — every gift triggers an animation in the room. Bigger gifts, bigger spectacle.
* **Gift tiers** — from a humble tofu cube to room-shaking legendary effects.
* **Visible generosity** — gift messages appear in chat with full flair. Generosity is a status symbol here.

### Red Envelopes

The classic crypto tradition, built into every room:

<!-- IMG: red envelope claim dialog screenshot -->

* Anyone can drop a **red envelope** into a room — a shared pool that the room races to claim.
* Random split: some claim dust, someone claims the jackpot. Speed and luck both matter.
* Perfect for celebrations: a big win, a clan victory, a token milestone, or pure chaos.

### The gift economy

Gifts and red envelopes run on platform points and tokens. Points can be earned through activity or topped up in the **Store**. Every transaction has instant visual feedback — sending, receiving, and claiming all feel like moments, not database writes.

***From shilling to gifting. Community has a currency now.***
