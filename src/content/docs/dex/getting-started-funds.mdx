Your trading account settles in **USDC**. Getting funds in and out is a first-class flow, not a scavenger hunt.

### Deposit

Open the **Deposit** dialog from the navbar (or your portfolio page):

* Deposit **USDC from Arbitrum** — the fastest, cheapest route into your trading account.
* The dialog shows your wallet balance, minimum deposit, and fee up front.
* Funds land in your trading account and are instantly available across all markets.

</div>

### Withdraw

Withdrawing takes your USDC back to your wallet on Arbitrum:

* Enter the amount, confirm in your wallet, done.
* The flat **0.2 USDC fee** is shown before you sign — no surprises.

### Transfer

**Transfer** moves funds between your own account buckets (e.g., perps collateral ↔ spot) — instant, free, and signed with a gasless signature.

  <img src={modalTransfer.src} alt="The Transfer dialog — move USDC between Hyperliquid Perps and Spot" />
</div>

### Send

**Send** lets you pay other people directly: send spot tokens from your account to **any Hyperliquid address** — a friend, a clanmate, whoever earned it.

* Instant and free — it's a gasless signature, not an on-chain transaction.
* Recent recipients are remembered, so paying your regulars takes seconds.
* The dialog guards against sending to system addresses, so a fat-fingered paste can't burn your tokens.

<div class="modal-shot">
  <img src={modalSend.src} alt="The Send on HyperCore dialog — instant and free to any Hyperliquid address" />
</div>

### EVM Transfer

Moving assets between your trading account and **HyperEVM** works in both directions from one dialog — pull tokens out to the EVM side, or bring them back to spot when it's time to trade. The network fee is paid in HYPE.

  <img src={modalEvm.src} alt="The EVM Transfer dialog — move assets between Spot and HyperEVM" />

### Bridge

Coming from another chain? The built-in **Bridge** page routes assets to Arbitrum USDC so you never leave the platform to top up.

***

*All balances, deposits, and withdrawals are visible in your [Portfolio](/dex/portfolio/) with full history.*
