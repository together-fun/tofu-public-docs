Three steps stand between you and your first HYPE: **Connect → Sign in → Enable trading**. The whole thing takes under a minute, and none of it costs gas.

### 1. Connect your wallet

Click **Connect** in the top-right corner and pick your weapon — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet, Rainbow, WalletConnect, and every major EVM wallet are supported. On mobile, a native connect sheet hands you straight to your wallet app.

<div class="modal-shot">
  <img src={connectModal.src} alt="The Connect a Wallet dialog — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, OKX Wallet, Rainbow, WalletConnect" />
</div>

Connecting is read-only and free: no transaction, no signature. You can browse every market, chatroom, and leaderboard before committing anything.

### 2. Sign in to TOFU

The moment your wallet connects, the setup checklist opens — **Connect ✓ → Sign in → Enable trading** — and parks on step 2. Hit **Sign in** and approve a single message in your wallet (a standard SIWE signature).

<div class="modal-shot">
  <img src={pipelineSignIn.src} alt="The setup checklist on step 2 — Sign in to TOFU. Wallet connected, sign once to trade with TOFU. Free signature, no gas." />
</div>

* **Free signature, no gas.** You're signing a message, not sending a transaction.
* This links your wallet to your TOFU identity: your level badge, avatar, XP, achievements, chat, and danmaku all hang off this sign-in.
* Declined by accident? Sign in whenever you're ready — the checklist waits.
* Already signed in with this wallet before? The checklist skips straight to step 3. All three done? It closes itself.

### 3. Enable trading

Before your first order, you'll be asked to **Enable Trading** — a one-time guided flow of up to four steps:

1. **Legal Check** — accept TOFU's Terms of Service. One tap, no signature.
2. **Account Activation** — your Hyperliquid account needs a deposit or past activity. Fresh wallet? Deposit any amount; funds stay in your own account.
3. **Approve TOFU Platform** — sign once to authorize TOFU as your builder. The builder fee cap is shown right in the dialog.
4. **Enable Trading Session** — create a **gas-free session key** for instant, low-latency trading.

<div class="modal-shot">
  <img src={enableTradingSession.src} alt="The Enable Trading on TOFU dialog — Legal, Activate, and Approve done; step 4 Enable Trading Session" />
</div>

* Every step is a **signature, not a transaction** — no gas cost.
* The session key means you don't have to sign every single order in your wallet. Trading stays one-click fast.
* Your funds always remain under your control.
* Not ready? Hit **Later**. **Enable Trading** stays one click away in your avatar menu until you finish it.
* Wallet says its agent slots are full? Use **Manage agents** to remove an old one, then retry.
