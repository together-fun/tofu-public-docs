# Token Cabal

Per-token mastery. Prove you're the whale of your coin.

<!-- IMG: cabal page screenshot -->

### What it is

The Token Cabal is TOFU's per-token progression system. Every token you trade tracks its own **token XP** — and climbing a token's cabal ranks makes you a recognized figure in that token's community.

### How it works

* Trading a token earns **token-specific XP** alongside your global XP.
* Milestones unlock escalating **cabal ranks** for that token.
* Your cabal progress is **public** — the room can see who the real ones are.

### Cabal privileges

Rank up in a token's cabal to unlock:

* **Token Badge** — a visual emblem on your profile and in chat showing your mastery of that token.
* **Room privileges** — elevated chat and danmaku rights in that token's room, plus entry announcements when you arrive.
* **Exclusive icon** — a unique symbol representing your bond with the token.

### Why it matters

Anyone can claim they're early. Cabal rank *proves* it — on-chain volume, tracked per token, displayed for everyone. It turns token communities into hierarchies of genuine conviction, and gives loyal traders the social status they've earned.
