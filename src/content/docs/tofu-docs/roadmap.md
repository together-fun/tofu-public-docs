Together.fun's development follows a deliberate three-phase strategy: from cultural hook, to trading revolution, to the ultimate degen playground.

| **Phase**                    | **Deliverables**                                                                                                                                                                                                                | **Status / Goal**                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Phase 1** — *Q2 2026*      | RugPad Royale on Solana mainnet: XP ranking, NFT characters, live in-game chat, avatars, achievements, social sharing.                                                                                                          | ✅ Completed. Acquired users, built community, established Together.fun as the home for degens.                                                                        |
| **Phase 2** — *Q3 2026*      | **The TOFU Trading Arcade**: gamified trading on Hyperliquid (spot, perps, outcome markets), live chatrooms & danmaku, gift economy, XP & levels, cosmetics & blind boxes, achievements, leaderboards, clan system, streaming. | 🚀 Live. A fully gamified trading platform — replacing complex interfaces with game mechanics, and solo trading with social competition.                              |
| **Phase 3** — *Q4 2026*      | Token Generation Event ($TOFU). Community distribution and liquidity mining. Unified identity across Season 1 and the Arcade. Deeper Game-Fi × Trade-Fi integration. Dedicated mobile app.                                      | Capture the entire value chain of the degen experience — from discovery and community to gaming and trading.                                                          |
