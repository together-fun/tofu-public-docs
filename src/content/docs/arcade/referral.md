Bring your people. Get paid for it.

<!-- IMG: referral page screenshot -->

### How it works

* Grab your **invite link** from the referral page and shill it to your network.
* When your invitees trade, you earn a share of their activity — automatically, forever.
* Track your invites, their activity, and your earnings in one dashboard.

### Stack it with Clans

Referrals are the entry level; **Clans** are the endgame. Invite enough people and you're not a referrer anymore — you're a clan leader with a treasury. See [Clans](/arcade/clans/) for the full creator economy.

***Your network is your net worth. Literally.***
