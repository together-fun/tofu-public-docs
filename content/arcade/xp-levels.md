# XP & Levels

Every trade earns XP. Every level tells the world who you are.

<!-- IMG: level page screenshot (level progress + rewards track) -->

### Earning XP

XP flows from everything you do on the platform:

* **Trading volume** — the core engine. More volume, more XP.
* **Achievements** — milestone unlocks come with XP payouts.
* **Engagement** — daily activity, social participation, events.

XP arrives in real time — you'll see it tick up as you trade, with a satisfying flourish.

### Levels

XP accumulates into your **level** — the single number that follows you everywhere:

* Displayed as a badge next to your name in chat, danmaku, leaderboards, and your profile.
* Higher levels unlock platform privileges: room permissions, event initiation rights, clan creation, and cosmetic tiers.
* Level rewards along the track: blind boxes, cosmetics, and points at key milestones.

### Chat level

Your social activity has its own progression — chat levels and badges that reward the community pillars, not just the volume whales. The anon who keeps the room alive at 3 AM deserves a badge too.

### Why it matters for TGE

XP isn't just cosmetic. As established in Season 1: **accumulated XP factors into the $TOFU airdrop allocation**. The grind pays off.
