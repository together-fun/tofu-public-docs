# Achievements

Milestones become badges. Badges become bragging rights.

<!-- IMG: achievement page screenshot -->

### How achievements work

Achievements track everything worth remembering across your TOFU career:

* **Trading milestones** — first trade, first $1K profit, volume tiers, consecutive green days.
* **Social milestones** — chat activity, gifts sent, rooms joined, community contribution.
* **Legendary moments** — including the dark ones. "Legendary Rekt" is an achievement. We celebrate scars here.
* **Collection milestones** — cosmetics collected, gallery completion, blind boxes opened.

### Rewards

Each achievement pays out — XP, points, blind boxes, or exclusive cosmetics that can't be obtained any other way. Achievement-locked cosmetics are the hardest flex on the platform: they can't be bought, only earned.

### Display

* Unlocked achievements show on your **public profile** for anyone to inspect.
* Key achievements grant **titles** displayed alongside your name.
* Progress bars show how close you are to the next unlock — grind visibly.

***Your worst trade and your best trade both end up on the wall.***
