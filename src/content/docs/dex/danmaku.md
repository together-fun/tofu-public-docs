Comments that fly across the chart. Price action becomes a crowd experience.

Danmaku (弹幕, "bullet comments") is TOFU's signature layer: real-time messages from the room streaming across the chart itself. When the candle rips, the screen fills with the crowd losing its mind. When it dumps... same, but redder.

![Danmaku flying across the live chart](../../../assets/screenshots/dex-danmaku.png)

### How it works

* Type your message in the danmaku bar and fire — it flies across the chart for everyone in the room, in real time.
* Danmaku renders on a dedicated overlay that **never blocks chart interaction** — you can keep drawing, zooming, and trading while the wall of text flows.
* Not your thing? Toggle danmaku off (or thin it out) any time. Focus mode is one click away.

### Danmaku styles

Danmaku is also a flex. Unlockable **danmaku styles** change how your messages look on everyone's screen:

* Colors, effects, and rarity-tiered styles earned from **Trading Drops** and platform events.
* Higher rarity hits harder: bigger text, heavier weight, glows, gradients — and Legendary even includes a **pinned** style that holds the top of the chart for a few seconds.
* Legendary styles are unmissable — when a Legendary danmaku crosses the chart, the room notices.
