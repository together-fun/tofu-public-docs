### Earning XP

XP flows from everything you do on the platform:

* **Trading volume** — the core engine. More volume, more XP.
* **Milestones** — reaching key marks pays out XP on top.
* **Engagement** — daily activity, social participation, events.

XP arrives in real time — you'll see it tick up as you trade, with a satisfying flourish.

### Levels

XP accumulates into your **level** — the single number that follows you everywhere:

* Displayed as a badge next to your name in chat, danmaku, leaderboards, and your profile.
* Higher levels unlock platform privileges: room permissions, cosmetic tiers, and (soon) clan creation.
* Level rewards along the track: reward packs at Lv5, 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 — the [Growth Route](/dex/achievements/#the-growth-route) — plus Trading Drops and points at key milestones.

### Chat level

Your level also maps to a **chat tier** — a badge displayed next to your name in every room, with a web3-native title to match. Twelve tiers cover the whole journey from **Paper Fish** (just connected) to **God Mode** (Lv.99+), and the badge **upgrades automatically** the moment you cross a threshold — no claiming. The badge does the talking: a Whale reads differently in chat than a Fresh Shrimp — and the anon who keeps the room alive at 3 AM climbs too, not just the volume whales. The full twelve-tier ladder lives at **More → Level** in the app.

### The long game

XP isn't just cosmetic. Everything you've earned — including back in the RugPad days — stays permanently on your record. We're not saying what it's for yet. We're saying the grind will be remembered. *More to come.*
