### Stream rooms

KOLs, top traders, and project teams can host live streams inside the platform. A stream room combines:

* **Live video** front and center.
* **Live chat** with the full identity layer — badges, frames, gifts.
* **The streamer's market context** — see what they're watching and trading.
* **Gifting** — support streamers directly; earnings come from gifts and engagement.

### PK mode

Two streamers, one screen, head-to-head. The room picks sides, gifts count as votes, and the loser faces the forfeit. Trading content meets battle content.

<!-- IMG: PK mode screenshot -->

### Multi-stream

Watch multiple streams in one view — for the terminally online who need every angle of the market covered at once.

### Discovery

Tokens with active streams get prioritized in discovery — a live room is a living market. Swipe through the feed to find where the action is.
