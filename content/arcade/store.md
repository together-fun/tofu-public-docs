### What's in the Store

* **Cosmetics** — a rotating selection of avatars, frames, nameplates, and danmaku styles available for direct purchase.
* **Blind boxes** — buy Trading Drops directly when the volume grind isn't fast enough.
* **Consumables** — rename cards, gift items, and utility props.
* **Points recharge** — top up platform points that power gifts, red envelopes, and purchases.

### Limited items

Watch for **Limited** tags: once an item is retired, it's gone from the store forever (existing owners keep theirs, fully usable). Retired items remain visible in the Gallery as monuments to those who were early.

### How pricing works

The store runs on platform points. Every item shows its price, rarity, and preview (animated cosmetics play their animation in the preview) before purchase. Purchases land in your Warehouse or Bags instantly.
